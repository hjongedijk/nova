# NOVA v0.2.11

Aura is now a real 3D figure instead of a moving picture.

- **Sculpted body.** A real 3D woman from our own sculpt: head, neck, sloped shoulders, upper arms, a fuller round bust and a slim waist. The face is blank, with no features.
- **Light.** The body is near-black with a glowing outline. Fine light lines flow from the neck over the collarbones and around the bust, in violet with gold accents, with star dust between them. Lines are drawn at the screen's own resolution, so they stay sharp on PC and phone.
- **Hair.** Long hair made of about 1,700 glowing strands that run from a centre parting to the tips, plus loose strands in the wind. The hair sways and follows the head with a slight delay.
- **Movement.** The head really turns toward the pointer and has small movements of its own. The chest breathes, and moods bloom from the face. On start she assembles from particles.
- **Sharpness.** Native resolution up to 2x on every device; phones no longer get a low-detail version.

The picture-based Aura and its image files are removed. The orb stays the default; choose Aura in the avatar settings. Reduced motion keeps everything still.

Known limits: the lines are still sparser than the look we are aiming for, the arms and face are plain, and frame time is not yet measured on a real graphics card.

Validation: web tests, svelte-check and the production build.
