# NOVA v0.2.12

Aura gets a new hologram look, a head that really turns, and a fuller bust.

- **Head.** The whole head and the hair on it now turn together toward the pointer, instead of only the face. The long hair follows with a delay, and the shoulders and chest turn a little with the head.
- **Body style.** The wire lines on the body are gone. The body is now a glowing shell, brightest at the outline, covered in fine star dust that twinkles and drifts over the skin. A soft band of light travels up her every few seconds.
- **Bust.** Fuller, and it now has its own soft movement when she turns, breathes or speaks.

Reduced motion keeps everything still. The orb stays the default; choose Aura in the avatar settings.

Known limits: the new movement was checked in still images and tests, not yet on a real graphics card. The chest between the outlines is fairly dark, and the face and arms are plain.

Validation: web tests, svelte-check and the production build.
