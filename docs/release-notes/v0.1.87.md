# NOVA v0.1.87

Removes the portrait's visible rectangular background cutoff. Source-image side edges dissolve into a procedural cyan/gold filament field that spans the viewport. The head retains its previous size; the renderer bounds its resolution on wide displays.

Adds visible idle head turns, tilts, connected neck movement and shoulder breathing. Listening leans the head forward; speech adds nods. Pointer tracking now listens passively at the window because the portrait mount intentionally ignores pointer events. Reduced-motion preferences disable the gestures and moving field. Cleanup removes the additional field and pointer listeners.

Validation covers edge fading, idle/listening/speech gestures, reduced motion, type checks, lint, web tests, production build, real browser motion and pointer response, viewport-wide desktop/mobile layouts, audio playback, shared face/orb selection and renderer disposal.
