# NOVA v0.1.89

Desktop wallpaper now renders the particle backdrop across the complete viewport, including its full height. A broader, curved cyan/gold field, faint haze and scattered lights replace the narrow horizontal band. The portrait remains centered above the composer, with its previous size and shared face/orb selection.

Replaces the fixed 640px render cap with a five-million-framebuffer-pixel budget, retaining more detail on large screens while bounding GPU memory. Smaller particle footprints reduce overlapping splats. Head turns are more pronounced, and the torso independently turns and sways around the lower chest. Both motions stop under reduced-motion preferences.

Validation: 437 web tests, Svelte checks, lint, production build and browser checks for full-resolution rendering, independent head/torso movement, reduced motion and renderer disposal. Deployment also verifies full-viewport wallpaper layout, desktop/mobile face/orb synchronization and actual audio playback.
