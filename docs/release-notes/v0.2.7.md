# NOVA v0.2.7

Aura v3: a bust portrait.

- **Framing.** Head, neck, shoulders and upper chest only. Below the collarbones the body dissolves into its own sparkle. At 16:9 the head is about 43% of the screen height; on a phone the view widens so both shoulders fit.
- **Face.** Calm closed eyes with fine lid lines, a slim nose, lips with a Cupid's bow and a soft chin. Porcelain shading with an inner glow, warm cheekbones and a fresnel rim, plus fine light veins at the temples and jaw. A slight turn and tilt at rest, with a small head sway.
- **Hair.** 288 guides in four layers, up to 8400 strands. Light travels down the strands, with a sheen at the crown and a few flyaways; hair stays off the face.
- **Light lines.** Branching magenta and peach curves over the neck, collarbones and chest, twinkling points where the lines meet, a breathing heart light and denser sparkle.
- Fixes: hair is no longer cut off behind the faded body, and the crease beside the nose is gone.

The orb stays the default. Validation: web tests (505), svelte-check.
