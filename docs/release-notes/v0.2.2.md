# NOVA v0.2.2

Gestalte gets a real upper body. The face is unchanged.

- **Anatomy.** The neck tapers with a hint of the neck muscles and flows into the trapezius, which slopes down to rounded shoulder caps; the upper arms go down and leave through the bottom edge instead of a flat plank running off the sides. Collarbone ridges, the hollow at the base of the throat, chest volume over a rib cage and a narrowing waist complete the bust.
- **Lines that wrap the body.** Torso lines are true slices of the 3D body seen from slightly above: they bow down over the chest, wrap the shoulders and circle the neck, and bunch up at the silhouette. The head fills 52% of the screen height to make room for the chest and arms.
- **Veins** run down the neck and fan out from the base of the throat over the collarbones and chest, without the bright band where neck and chest met.

The orb stays the default. Validation: svelte-check, web tests (466), lint, Prettier and the production build.
