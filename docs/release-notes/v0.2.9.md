# NOVA v0.2.9

Aura v5: new artwork and flowing light lines.

- **New artwork.** A new owner-approved picture (FLUX.1 schnell on the owner's PC) with a blank head, a short natural neck, a feminine bust and full light-strand hair. Head, hair and body are now measured from the picture, which removes the shoulder seam.
- **Flowing light lines.** 35 hand-shaped curves with gentle S-bends run along the neck, collarbones, shoulders, arms, bust contours, flanks and belly. Each has a pink-white core, a magenta and violet glow, peach accents, tapered ends, light travelling along it, sparks and star points. The face gets only three faint lines and no features.
- **Grade.** Deep violet and magenta with the blue cast removed, darker glossy skin with starry dust, boosted hair strands, and a darker room with soft smoke and drifting ribbons.
- **Head turn.** No more stretching: the unseen side of the head shows dark skin.

The orb stays the default. Validation: web tests (512), svelte-check and the production build.
