# NOVA v0.2.3

Gestalte v5: head, body and background reworked from research into holographic figures (techniques only, nothing copied).

- **Head.** A sculpted form with brow ridge, soft socket hollows, nose bridge, cheekbones and jaw angle; hollows read as shade instead of glow. Line weight follows the facing ratio: hairlines on the front, heavier toward the silhouette, and lines further back are thinner, dimmer and bluer.
- **Veins** branch like lightning: they kink but never loop, fork and taper, and cool from a hot trunk to amber branches and blue twigs.
- **Sparks** drift on curl noise, defocus into soft discs and cool from white to blue with age.
- **Background.** Three far ridge layers with atmospheric haze, a nebula and two twinkling star layers kept off the figure, a soft back glow with slow rays, and finer dust on the landscape. The gaze moves the layers with parallax.
- **Film finish.** One cheap post pass for edge chromatic aberration, grading, vignette, grain, faint scanlines and a light shimmer.
- **Motion.** Head pose on damped springs with a small sway, and the chest breathes. Reduced motion freezes all of it.

The orb stays the default. Validation: svelte-check, web tests (466), lint, Prettier and the production build.
