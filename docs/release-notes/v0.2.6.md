# NOVA v0.2.6

Gestalte v6: the figure rebuilt as light dots (an original, procedural design).

- **Point-cloud figure.** About 95k dots along contour bands that wrap the body, with dark gaps between them so the form reads. The rim is saturated cyan and the interior a dim deep blue. Amber lightning veins run down the neck, and dust drifts off the crown and shoulders.
- **Face fire.** A turbulent amber glow in the face instead of an orb, with shaded eye sockets.
- **Land.** Dotted mountain ridges in depth (about 115k points) with amber lava veins and about 200 slow ridge lights, on a near-black sky with sparse stars.
- **Moods bloom from the face.** The dots never move; the new mood spreads outward from the face with a soft glowing edge. Speaking brightness follows the audio level.
- **Birth.** A river of dots flows out of the orb and builds the body from the bottom up. The face ignites when the dome of the head closes, then one shockwave crosses the figure and lights up the land. The last frame is exactly the rest pose. The geometry is built in a Web Worker, so chat stays responsive.
- **Head and breath.** The head follows the pointer on a smooth spring, the side turning away darkens, and the chest breathes. Reduced motion shows a still rest pose.
- **Quality.** A quality tier is picked from the GPU, and long frames first draw fewer dots, then lower the resolution.

The orb stays the default. Validation: web tests (505), svelte-check and the production build.
