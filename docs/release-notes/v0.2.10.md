# NOVA v0.2.10

Aura v6: more lines, hair in the wind and a living head.

- **99 flowing light lines.** Our own composition.
  - **Chest:** the richest part. Sweeps run from the collarbones over and around a rounder bust, with fine companion strands and warm peach and amber undersides, plus concentrated sparkle.
  - **Arms:** calmer, with their own rim light and a dark gap to the torso, so they read as limbs.
  - **Face:** 9 faint lines that follow the head's form, with no features and no centre seam.
- **Hair.** Toned toward violet. The crown flows into the long hair through strands from the parting. The hair sways in a gentle wind with gusts and flutter at the tips, lags behind head movement with a small overshoot, and lifts slightly while speaking.
- **Head.** Subtle idle motion and an occasional look aside. Listening adds a tilt, thinking a tilt and an upward drift, and speaking adds nods in time with the voice. Head turns use parallax-occlusion marching, so lines no longer zigzag.
- **Glow.** Line halos no longer leave hard dark edges.

Reduced motion keeps everything still. The orb stays the default. Validation: web tests (517), svelte-check and the production build.
