# NOVA v0.1.93

Makes HUD sidebars readable over bright scenery with dark panel backing, brighter text, larger type and more usable width. Raises the portrait framebuffer budget from five to nine million pixels, allowing native 3840×2160 rendering, and adds restrained texture sharpening while preserving mipmap filtering.

The humanoid and mountain field now follow NOVA's existing state palette: gold when listening, violet when thinking, green when executing and pink when speaking. Ready retains the cyan/gold artwork. Transitions are smoothed and actual speech energy still drives brightness.

Separates the moving humanoid from the mountain artwork with a shared silhouette mask. The mountain texture has no animated UV offset and stays fixed while the head and torso turn. Orthographic turn compensation counteracts foreshortening so turns do not visibly shrink the portrait.

Validation: Svelte checks, focused lint, production build, real GPU browser checks at 3840×2160, five distinct state colors, reduced motion, head/torso movement and real audio playback with speech tint. In two sampled turning frames, head width varied by less than 1%; the scenery patch differed by only 0.33 on the 0–255 channel scale. Browser checks also verify sidebar backing, input access, full-page framing and shared face/orb preferences. No production mutations are made by the checks.
