# NOVA v0.2.8

Aura v4: built from approved artwork.

- **Artwork as the figure.** Aura now starts from an owner-approved reference picture: a feminine bust with a blank, smooth head and long hair of glowing light strands. It was generated with FLUX.1 schnell (Apache-2.0) on the owner's own PC. At rest the figure matches the picture; it is rebuilt from up to 280k image-sampled particles.
- **Neon star stripes.** Stripes of twinkling light dots run over the blank head and the body. They follow the rounded form, with a white core, a coloured glow halo, star points and ends that break into stardust. Colours are mostly violet and magenta with pink, cyan and peach accents, and light travels slowly along them. No facial features are drawn.
- **Moods.** Each state blooms out from the face with its own palette. Speaking brightness follows the audio level.
- **Birth and motion.** At start, particles flow out of a glow on the chest and settle into place; the face ignites last, followed by a shockwave. The head turns toward the pointer as a depth illusion, the chest breathes, the hair shimmers and the ribbons carry slow lights.
- **Fallbacks.** On a phone the view zooms to the bust. Without WebGL2 the still picture is shown with a soft glow. Reduced motion shows a still rest pose.

The orb stays the default. Validation: web tests (511), svelte-check and the production build.
