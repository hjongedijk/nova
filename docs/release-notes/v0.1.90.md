# NOVA v0.1.90

Fixes the overlapping-dot and moiré artifacts visible while the holographic portrait turns. The same source artwork now covers a continuous shallow surface, with mipmap and anisotropic filtering, instead of independent point sprites. Fine facial and shoulder contours remain connected through gestures. Head yaw and pitch are bounded when idle, pointer and speech movement combine, preventing excessive distortion of the inferred depth.

Head/torso movement, full-page wallpaper effects, shared face/orb preferences, speech energy and reduced-motion behavior remain available. Disposing the portrait releases its texture as well as its geometry and material.

Validation: 438 web tests, Svelte checks, lint, production build and browser checks of turning head/torso poses, full-resolution rendering, reduced motion, cleanup and real audio playback. Production deployment verifies desktop/mobile settings and full-page wallpaper coverage.
